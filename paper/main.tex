  {\LARGE A Neural-Network Engine for International Draughts\\[0.3em]
  that Learns from a Teacher and from Its Losses\par}
  \vspace{1.5cm}
  {\large Marwin Steiner\par}
  \vspace{0.5cm}
  {\large \today\par}
  \vspace{2cm}
  \begin{minipage}{0.85\textwidth}
    \centering \textbf{Abstract}\\[0.5em]
    \raggedright
    We present an engine for international draughts that combines an alpha-beta
    search with a small neural network for judging positions. Playing rated blitz
    games against people and other programs on the public server lidraughts.org,
    it reached a rating of 2459, higher than 99.98\% of the site's blitz players.
    In 76 games against human opponents it never lost.
  \end{minipage}
  \vfill
\end{titlepage}
